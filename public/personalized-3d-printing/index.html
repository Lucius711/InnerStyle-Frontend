What is personalized 3D printing?

Personalized 3D printing means making a one-of-a-kind 3D object designed around you — a portrait figurine, a character figure, a chibi, your pet, a brand mascot — instead of buying an off-the-shelf model. The traditional way is to send photos to a print shop, wait for an artist to sculpt a model, and go back and forth on quotes for days. InnerStyle cuts that out: AI builds the model instantly, and you review and edit it yourself before printing.

How personalized 3D printing works at InnerStyle

  1. Upload a photo or describe it — one sharp photo, or a few lines describing your character.
  2. AI builds the 3D model — a full-color, textured 3D model in minutes.
  3. Preview & edit — rotate 360°, view it in AR, tweak colors and materials, add a base.
  4. Printability check — the model is analyzed and automatically repaired to be watertight and printable.
  5. Order & receive — pick 8, 12 or 15 cm, enter your address, pay by QR, track your order online.
